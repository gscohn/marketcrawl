How to start

  1. Send a direct message to @sportscrawl.app on Bluesky. Anything will do, so hello is fine.
  2. It replies with a link. Pick your teams and the time you want the digest to arrive.
  3. That's it. The first digest arrives at your chosen time.

What you get

  • MLB, NBA, WNBA, NFL, NHL, MLS, NWSL and the Premier League, plus the World Cup, Champions League and Concacaf competitions.
  • One message, at the time you choose, in your timezone.
  • Silence on days your teams don't play. An NFL-only subscriber hears nothing from February to September.

Stopping

DM stop to pause and keep your settings, or delete to remove everything. Both work immediately, and delete asks once before it does anything irreversible.

SportsCrawl is a personal project with no company behind it and no uptime guarantee. It stores your Bluesky account id, your timezone, your delivery time and your team choices, and nothing else. The privacy policy says exactly what that means.
